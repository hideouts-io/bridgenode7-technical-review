This is a working plan, not a scheduled automation. The initial public audit has already established the inventory, pinned source revisions, baseline checks, and findings referenced below. The external reviewer can reproduce findings, refine the review report, and prepare candidate changes. Calendar completion depends on maintainer availability; waiting for access does not count as an implemented improvement.

## Decisions that keep the week achievable

The public inventory and review handoff preparation are complete: seven applicable patches and an evaluation-only FMA specification have recorded verification, source bases and responsible roles. The 28 hours remain a planning estimate, not measured execution time. The next company step is maintainer review of T01–T03, with explicit owner decisions for FDE's supported timestamp policy, Pax's actual provenance/optional contract and Quantum's scope/counting/evidence rules. [TODO.md](TODO.md) owns those actions and acceptance criteria. Upstream implementation, role assignment and release approval remain open.

- **First:** prioritize T01, T02, and T03 because they affect what validators or portable outputs allow a reviewer to trust. Use T04 as a small, independently testable onboarding improvement.
- **Before data correction:** the Pax evidence owner must establish the actual eligibility verification date and appropriate snapshot boundary. A stricter validator should expose the current inconsistency rather than conceal it.
- **Before expanding research:** complete the quantum example's existing method before adding new domains, scores, or algorithms. Its readiness profile remains a governance method, not a cryptographic implementation or operational certification.
- **Before release:** passing the local candidate checks is an input to the maintainer's decision. It does not authorize a release, deployment, or claim of production readiness.

## Completion and deferred scope

The external review week succeeds when every accepted task has a proposed responsible role, a reviewable minimal change or specification, and verified evidence or explicitly pending acceptance checks from `TODO.md`. Implementation completion additionally requires the maintainer's acceptance evidence. Rejected or deferred tasks need a reason and a concrete dependency; they do not need another document or control framework.

The following remain outside this public review: private repositories, internal deployments, real protected evidence, customer or supplier qualification, scientific validation of frontier-technology claims, operational cryptographic changes, and a comprehensive dependency or security assessment. If new public repositories appear or an inspected contract changes, update the inventory and recheck the affected relationship before carrying the corresponding proposal forward.
